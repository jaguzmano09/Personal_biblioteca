## Jurisdiction and notice drafting

- For the confirmed Colombia launch, review Law 1581 and its implementing rules for notice, authorization, demonstrability and rights requests. Decree 1074 specifies policy content in Article 2.2.2.25.3.1 and special protections for minors in Article 2.2.2.25.2.9. [Law 1581](https://normograma.mintic.gov.co/mintic/compilacion/docs/ley_1581_2012.htm), [Decree 1074](https://normograma.mintic.gov.co/mintic/compilacion/docs/decreto_1074_2015.htm)
- If the operator is established in the EU, offers services to people there, or monitors their behavior there, assess GDPR territorial scope and the relevant notice, rights and transfer provisions with qualified counsel. Verify any other markets independently. [GDPR, Article 3](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)
- Inventory actual cookies, web storage and platform features before deciding whether a consent control is needed. Verify the applicable legal grounds, child-data rules, transfers and processor retention before drafting publication-ready documents.
